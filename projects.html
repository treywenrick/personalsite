Project Overview

This class project valued Microsoft through a comparable-company framework centered on cloud computing, enterprise software, and AI exposure.

I combined recent company news, industry risks, competitive positioning, and valuation multiples into one investment recommendation that argued Microsoft was undervalued relative to its long-term fundamentals.

Comparable Companies

  • Alphabet
  • Oracle
  • Nvidia
  • Adobe
  • Visa
  • SAP

Methods Used

  • P/E with a median multiple of 33.9x
  • P/OCF with a median multiple of 25.0x
  • EV/Sales with a median multiple of 8.5x
  • Weighted blend of 50%, 30%, and 20%

Investment Thesis

The project emphasized Microsoft’s cloud leadership, strong enterprise relationships, diversified revenue base, and early AI monetization through Azure and Copilot.

Risks

  • Heavy AI-related capex pressuring margins
  • Regulatory scrutiny in AI and cloud markets
  • Execution risk around large-scale infrastructure builds

Catalysts

  • Expansion of Azure and cloud market share
  • Monetization of Copilot and AI products
  • Broader enterprise adoption of AI productivity tools
